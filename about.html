This site acts as a repository for selected works on sound art theory. Sound art has historically been less understood than physical works and this webzine is dedicated to the critical and theoretical exploration of the auditory medium. Through articles, video works and theoretical pieces from artists such as John Cage, Volkmar Klien and more, this space investigates the ideas, aesthetics and histories that have no doubt shaped the field and questions the boundaries where music composition dissolves into art.

Sound art sits at a peculiar intersection of the art world — ephemeral and temporal, sound art has long been resistant to the traditional frameworks of fine art making the field more difficult to evaluate and analyze. I am interested in the tension that persists in the lines between music and sound art, what it means to listen critically and how silence functions as a conceptual tool.
