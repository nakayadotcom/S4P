Can you hear the room now (in A minor?)

This project remaps the unnoticed frequencies of your environment into an A minor harmonic scale. By driving sine and sawtooth oscillators with the room's ambient noise, it translates the static of the background into a presence that allows you to engage with the unnoticed.
